A Home Away From Home In The Heart Of Ikeja

The Leabridge School (Ikeja) was founded by a couple with two young children of their own, who wanted to combine their passion for teaching with real, practical support for working parents. That idea shapes everything we do — we see ourselves as a genuine "Home Away From Home" for every child who walks through our doors.

Today, Leabridge is a private creche, pre-school and primary school for children from 3 months to 5 years and beyond, blending British, Montessori and Nigerian curricula. We were recognised as one of the top 20 primary schools in Lagos in an independent poll — a reflection of the care our teachers bring to the classroom every day.
