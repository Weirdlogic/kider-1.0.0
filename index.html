A Home Away From Home In The Heart Of Ikeja

The Leabridge School was founded by a couple who wanted to combine their passion for teaching with real, practical support for working parents. What began as a simple idea has grown into one of Ikeja's most loved early years and primary schools.

We blend British, Montessori and Nigerian curricula across our creche, toddler class, play school and primary programmes, with small class sizes and a daily report on every child's activities — so you're always close to what your little one has been up to.
