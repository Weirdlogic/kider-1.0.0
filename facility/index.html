Why Families Choose Leabridge

From our blended curriculum to daily updates on your child's day, here's what makes Leabridge a true home away from home.

Blended Curriculum

A thoughtful mix of British, Montessori & Nigerian curricula, tailored to every stage of early learning through to primary school.

Outdoor Play

Safe, guided outdoor play that builds confidence, motor skills and a love of the outdoors.

Daily Parent Reports

A report on your child's activities is sent home every day, so you're never far from their world.

Home Away From Home

Small classes and warm, attentive caregivers, with around 1 teacher for every 5 children in our early years.
